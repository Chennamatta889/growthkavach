Your toolkit

SEO markup checker

Paste a page’s HTML source to check basic on-page SEO. This is a markup inspection, not a live crawl or Google ranking test.

Checks title, description, headings, canonical, noindex, image alternative text and declared language. Pasted HTML is never rendered or executed.

Google snippet preview

Write concise search result titles and descriptions. Google may choose to display different text.

Campaign link builder

Create tagged links to identify visitors from social media, newsletters and campaigns in your analytics.

Analytics must be installed on the destination website to measure campaign traffic.

Marketing ROI calculator

Estimate the economics of a campaign in rupees, using your actual or hypothetical inputs.

ROI = (revenue − product/service costs − all marketing costs) ÷ all marketing costs. ROAS = revenue ÷ ad spend. CPA and CPL use total marketing costs. Excludes unentered costs and taxes.

Keyword idea builder

Turn a topic into search-oriented ideas. These are generated suggestions, not measured search volume or ranking difficulty.

Validate demand in Search Console or a licensed keyword research service before publishing.

Content planner

Generate a practical weekly publishing plan from a topic. This uses local templates, not AI or live trend data.
